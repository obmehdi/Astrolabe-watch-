# Rabat Islamic Astrolabe

A fully offline astrolabe and astronomical watch for Rabat, Morocco (34.02° N, 6.84° W).
Pure HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG and canvas. No server, API or libraries. Labels in English, French and Arabic (RTL).

## Two modes

**Instrument.** An interactive astrolabe: rete with named stars, tympan with altitude and azimuth lines, rotating rule, Sun on the ecliptic, and a back side. The sky rotates with local sidereal time.

**Watch.** A luxury-style astronomical watch face:
- rose-gold case, ceramic bezel and a 24-hour ring
- planisphere with 72 constellation figures, names and bright stars
- 3D granulated Sun with prominences
- 3D textured Moon with its exact phase, drawn next to the Sun
- horizon window (S at the top, E on the left, as on the instrument)
- **Moon cycle:** play, pause, step by day, slider or date picker, to see the Moon phase on any date

## Prayer times
Ministry of Habous method for Morocco: Fajr 19°, Isha 17°, Asr standard shadow, Dhuhr +5 min, Maghrib +5 min, sunrise −3 min. Checked against the published Rabat timetable for 15–18 Oct 2026, every prayer within about one minute.

Morocco has been on GMT (UTC+0) since 20 Sep 2026. Some browsers still use an older time-zone table, so the app forces GMT for Rabat and Laayoune after that date. You can change this by tapping the time-zone pill.

## Also included
Qibla, star list, calendar and Hijri date (approximate), shadow tool, and printable A4/A3 plates.

## Install (PWA)
Open the page in Chrome on your phone, then use the menu and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen". It works offline after the first load.

Host it on GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > deploy from the main branch, root folder.

## Android app and widget
The `android/` folder (if included) wraps the same page in an Android app with a home-screen widget that shows only the watch circle. Build it from GitHub Actions or Android Studio. Widgets refresh about every 15 minutes.

## Accuracy and limits
- Sun: NOAA/Meeus formulas. Moon: truncated Meeus series (phase and illumination, not for eclipse prediction).
- Sun and Moon textures are generated by code, not photographs.
- The watch star map covers the northern-hemisphere sky as seen from Rabat.
- The astrolabe is an educational instrument. Check prayer times against your local mosque or the Ministry of Habous before relying on them.
